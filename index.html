About these figures

State color is the documented subtotal for 2014–29 Oct 2026. Larger amounts are darker green in the light theme and brighter green in the dark theme. A hatched state has a record and no total. That blank is not zero.

The $41.04 billion sums sourced, state-specific project commitments, completed project totals and acquisition prices. It is not foreign direct investment (FDI) stock, an annual investment flow, or a verified transfer from Japan. Announced scope does not establish how much was spent. Unallocated deals and presence-only records are excluded from the subtotal.

Sources include company releases and reports, state and local government announcements, regulatory filings and public records. Each record links to its source. Full project scopes are counted once; missing state amounts remain blank. This is a documented ledger, not a complete census of Japanese investment. Click a company to see its other U.S. states.

Flow arcs are corporate links between a Japanese parent office and a U.S. project, headquarters or state reference. Moving dots show direction of investment, not traced bank transfers.

Map: U.S. Census / Natural Earth state shapes via us-atlas; Natural Earth Japan outline via world-atlas; drawn with D3. Fonts: Inter and Newsreader, under the SIL Open Font License.
