About these figures

These totals add selected, sourced amounts involving Japanese firms from 2014 through 29 October 2026. The search looked for strong primary-source examples. It is not a census of every investment.

The $41.036 billion is a documented subtotal. It is not Japan’s cash contribution, not state foreign-direct-investment stock, and not a verified transfer from a Japanese bank. Figures are nominal, not inflation-adjusted.

Color is that state subtotal. A blank amount stays undisclosed — it is not zero. Ten states have a record and no state total. Announced commitments, completed projects, and acquisitions are different measures; each record keeps its own.

Some amounts are whole project budgets shared with partners. An arc links a parent office to a U.S. project, headquarters, or state reference. It is a corporate link, not a financing route.
