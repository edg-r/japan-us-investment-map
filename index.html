Scope, sources, and amount definitions

This is a selected, source-backed ledger. It is not a complete census or total foreign direct investment stock by state. Project budgets, acquisition prices, loans, and equity investments cannot be added into a comparable stock measure. Joint venture totals may include U.S. or other foreign partners. Announced spending is not proof of expenditure. Ownership changes between Japanese-affiliated owners are not automatically net new foreign investment. Status follows the cited source unless a later change is explicitly verified.

Japanese points use parent-office cities documented in company profiles. Coordinates are approximate city references from Natural Earth or the R maps city database. U.S. boundaries: Census Bureau, 2024, 1:20 million. World boundaries: Natural Earth, 1:110 million. The source and ownership profile for each selected record are linked above.

Solid routes: a record with a state-specific reported amount. Dashed routes: an undisclosed or geographically unallocated amount. Existing presence and cooperation agreements have reference markers and no animated investment route. An open marker does not mean zero investment.
